Interpretation

Decision evidence
Engineering result

Bounded memory, exact seam reconciliation, deterministic resume, reusable HDF5 caches, and a 38.5% warm-run speed improvement all succeeded.

Scientific result

Best-fixed female accuracy falls by 9.59 percentage points, observed female-ID changes rise 7.4×, and direct all-three coverage falls by 8.73 points.

Ground-truth limitation

The ledger labels only the female. These metrics cannot independently detect swaps between the two males, so full three-fish identity truth remains an acceptance requirement.
